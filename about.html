About

Training nobody can sleep through.

Nobody should get hurt at work because training was boring, unclear, or never tracked. Atlas Labs exists to kill that excuse.

Mission

We turn every warehouse floor into a place a new starter can learn in minutes: look around a real 360 scene, face the actual hazards, prove it in a scored quiz, and walk out certified. Managers see proof, not paperwork.

Story

Atlas Labs is a six member student software team formed for the CET257 Enterprise Project at the University of Sunderland. First deployment: Colab Warehouse starter induction. Method: plain words, real proof, no one left untrained.
